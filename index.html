Meet the Artist

Darla Jane Thornock

Visual Artist · Illustrator · Creative Designer

I’m a multidisciplinary artist and designer based in Bozeman, Montana, where I build and sell my own original work.

Since 2018 I’ve worked as an independent visual artist, selling original paintings and taking on commissions, from the first conversation with a client through branding and final delivery. Over the same years I’ve worked as a freelance digital illustrator, creating custom illustrations and digital assets for clients and Web3 platforms, including NFT design.

My eye for images also comes from working on set. Since 2016 I’ve modeled and collaborated on fashion and editorial shoots, and since 2019 I’ve sourced vintage and styled pieces as a set and wardrobe designer, building mood boards and cohesive visual stories for shoots and campaigns.

I’m entrepreneurial, visually driven, and highly self-managed, and I’m glad to work remotely. I’m always happy to talk about commissions and illustration projects.
